About Me

I am a Ph.D. student working with Dr. Jaichander Swaminathan in the Energy Research Centre at IISc. My research focuses on the thermodynamic optimal design of energy systems, particularly desalination processes for brine concentration. My previous research experience includes investigating the impact of climate change on heating and cooling energy demands, optimizing fuel cell systems, and designing solar simulators.

Research Interests

  • Brine concentration
  • Vapor Compression Processes
