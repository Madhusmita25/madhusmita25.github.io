About Me

I am a Ph.D. student working with Dr. Jaichander Swaminathan in the Energy Research Centre at IISc Bengaluru. My research focuses on thermodynamic optimal design of vapor compression desalination systems. I have previously worked on understanding the impact of climate change on heating/cooling energy demand, fuel cell systems optimization, and solar simulator design.
